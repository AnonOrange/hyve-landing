📖 Hyve Sleuth — How to Use

🔎 What is Hyve Sleuth?

Hyve Sleuth is an OSINT research launcher built by Vibe Software Solutions / Majixx Corp. It doesn't scrape data — it opens official public records and research databases in new browser tabs, pre-filled with your subject's information wherever possible. Think of it as a smart command center for investigators.

🚀 Quick Start — 4 Steps

1
Create a Researcher Profile — Click your profile name in the header to manage profiles. Each profile keeps its own saved subjects, notes, and findings separate.
2
Fill in the Subject Profile — Enter as much information as you have. At minimum, enter a Full Name. The more fields you fill, the more SMART links activate.
3
Click Load — This activates the full resource panel. Use the tabs to navigate by category (Courts, People, Email, etc.). Gold-highlighted cards are Smart Links — they open pre-filled search forms.
4
Launch & Research — Click any card to open it in a new tab. Use ⚡ Launch All Smart to open every pre-filled link at once. Use per-section ⚡ Launch (N) to batch-open just one category.

🔑 Smart Links Explained

Cards marked SMART use your subject's info to pre-populate the destination URL — saving you from manually typing into each site. The more inputs you fill, the more smart links activate.

Name → {name}People search, court records, news
Email → {email}Breach checks, HIBP, email rep
Phone → {phone}Reverse lookup, carrier ID
Username → {username}WhatsMyName, IDCrawl, social
Image URL → {image}Google Lens, TinEye, Yandex
VIN → {vin}NHTSA, CARFAX, VehicleHistory
Domain/IP → {domain}WHOIS, Shodan, VirusTotal
State → {state}Unlocks all state-specific tabs

✅ Tracking Your Findings

Each resource card has two small buttons on the right:

  • ✓ (Found) — Mark this resource as a hit. The card turns green. Findings are saved per subject, per profile.
  • ✕ (No Result) — Mark as no result. The card dims. Helps you track which resources you've already checked.

Hit/Miss data is stored locally in your browser — nothing leaves your device.

📝 Research Notes & Export

  • Use the Research Notes box in the sidebar to jot down findings for the loaded subject. Click Save Notes to persist them.
  • Click 📄 Export Findings (sidebar or header) to download a .txt report with subject info, hit/miss results, and your notes.
  • Use Save to store the subject profile for quick re-loading later.
